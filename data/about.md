## Purpose

PH Law Atlas makes a curated collection of public-health case law easier to browse, search, filter, and explore. It is designed primarily for Medical Officers of Health, Associate Medical Officers of Health, public-health legal and policy staff, and other professionals who need rapid access to jurisprudence relevant to public-health practice.

PH Law Atlas is a **personal, independent project**. It is not produced by, affiliated with, or endorsed by any public health unit, board of health, government body, CanLII, or other organization.

## What the atlas contains

The collection focuses on Ontario public-health cases and other legal authorities relevant to public-health practice. Each case page presents source metadata and a curated case card, with links to the original decision and official legislation where available.

The Markdown case cards maintained in the repository are the source data used by the website. They are **not authoritative legal sources**. The original court or tribunal decision and the applicable legislation should always be consulted where accuracy or legal interpretation matters.

The collection is selective rather than comprehensive. The omission of a case, statute, topic, or classification should not be interpreted as a statement about its legal importance or relevance.

## How to use it

Use search to look across case titles, citations, summaries, topics, public-health functions, legislation, statutory provisions, organizations, and roles. The Explore page provides additional ways to browse the collection using existing case metadata.

PH Law Atlas is intended to help users **find and understand potentially relevant authorities more quickly**. It is not intended to replace CanLII, official legal sources, independent legal research, or advice from legal counsel.

## Editorial approach

Case cards are maintained as structured Markdown files with editorial metadata. Relationships, classifications, legislation references, and other metadata are displayed only where they have been recorded in the source material. Missing information is not inferred by the application.

**Artificial intelligence tools have been used to assist with the initial drafting of some case summaries and metadata**. AI-generated material may subsequently be revised by a human, but users should not assume that a case card has been independently verified or reviewed by legal counsel unless this is expressly indicated.

The case summaries and other editorial content in PH Law Atlas **have not been legally reviewed or approved and should not be relied upon as legal advice**. AI-assisted drafting can introduce errors, omit relevant considerations, or overstate or misunderstand the significance of a decision.

The project may be updated as additional cases are identified or existing entries are revised. It should not be assumed that a case card reflects all subsequent judicial consideration, legislative amendments, or other developments affecting the law.

## Important notice

**PH Law Atlas is a reference and research aid, not legal advice**.

Nothing on this site should be relied upon as a legal opinion or as a substitute for reviewing the underlying authorities and obtaining legal advice where appropriate. Case summaries are necessarily selective and may contain errors or omissions.

The original court or tribunal decisions, official legislation, and other primary legal sources remain authoritative.
